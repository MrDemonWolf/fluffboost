## Is FluffBoost free?

Yes. The daily quote, `/quote`, and community suggestions are free. Premium adds
[custom scheduling](/docs/premium).

## The quote didn't show up — what happened?

Run through these:

- Did you set a channel with `/setup channel`?
- Can FluffBoost **view the channel**, **send messages**, and **embed links** there?
- Is it a normal **text channel** (not forum/voice/category)?
- Is the current time actually your scheduled time in your timezone?

<Callout type="warn">
  If FluffBoost's role can't view the channel, the daily post fails silently.
  Check the channel's permissions for the bot's role first.
</Callout>

## Where do the quotes come from?

A starter library plus community suggestions reviewed by the FluffBoost team.
The approved library is shared across servers. See [Suggestions](/docs/suggestions).

## Can I change the delivery time?

The free schedule is daily at 8:00 AM America/Chicago. Custom times, frequencies,
and timezones are a [premium](/docs/premium) feature via `/setup schedule`.

## What data does FluffBoost store?

Server IDs, channel and schedule settings, Premium status, and quote submissions
with submitter/reviewer user IDs. Operational logs can include command names,
usernames, and IDs. The bot does not monitor conversations; suggestion submitters
may receive a review-result DM. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details
and deletion requests.

## How do I get help?

Join the [community Discord](https://mrdwolf.net/discord) — the fastest way to
reach the team and other server owners.
