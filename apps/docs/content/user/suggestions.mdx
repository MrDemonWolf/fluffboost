title: Suggest & review quotes

FluffBoost's library is shared across servers. Members can suggest a quote;
the FluffBoost team decides what joins the library. Approved submissions can
be delivered to **any server using the bot**, with author and submitter attribution.

## Suggest a quote (anyone)

```text
/suggestion quote:"Rest is productive too." author:"MossyPaws"
```

Quote and author are both required. The suggestion goes to the bot team's review
channel, where your Discord profile is shown with the submission. When it is
reviewed, FluffBoost may send you a direct message with the outcome.

A few limits keep the queue manageable:

- Up to **3 suggestions every 24 hours** per person, across all servers.
- The quote can be up to **1,024 characters** and the author up to **256**.
- `/suggestion` works inside a server, not in direct messages with the bot.

## Review suggestions (bot team)

Users authorized by the bot operator manage the shared queue with
`/admin suggestion` commands. Your own server's Administrator permission does
not grant access to this queue.

| Command                        | What it does                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| `/admin suggestion list`       | List suggestions                      |
| `/admin suggestion approve`    | Approve a suggestion into the library |
| `/admin suggestion reject`     | Decline a suggestion                  |
| `/admin suggestion stats`      | See suggestion counts at a glance     |

The list is sorted newest first and includes up to 500 suggestions per page.
Use the optional `status` filter and `page` number to browse older or reviewed
suggestions. Without a status filter, the list includes pending, approved, and
rejected suggestions.

<Callout type="info">
  Approved quotes join the same shared pool used for daily delivery and
  `/quote`. There is no private, per-server quote library.
</Callout>

## Tips for good suggestions

- Keep it short and genuinely encouraging.
- Include accurate attribution; use your chosen name for your own original quote.
- Submit content you have permission to share and keep private information out.
- Write for the wider community — your quote may appear in someone else's server.
