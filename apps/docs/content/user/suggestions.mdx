---
title: Suggest & review quotes
description: Let members propose quotes and give admins a simple review queue.
---

FluffBoost's quote library grows with your community. Anyone can suggest a
quote; admins decide what makes the cut.

## Suggest a quote (anyone)

```text
/suggestion quote:"Rest is productive too." author:"MossyPaws"
```

The suggestion goes into a pending review queue — it isn't posted until an admin
approves it.

## Review suggestions (admins)

Admins manage the queue with the `/admin suggestion` commands:

| Command                        | What it does                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| `/admin suggestion list`       | Show pending suggestions              |
| `/admin suggestion approve`    | Approve a suggestion into the library |
| `/admin suggestion reject`     | Decline a suggestion                  |
| `/admin suggestion stats`      | See suggestion counts at a glance     |

<Callout type="info">
  Approved quotes join the same pool the daily quote is drawn from, so your
  server's voice shows up in the morning rotation over time.
</Callout>

## Tips for good suggestions

- Keep it short and genuinely encouraging.
- Attribute it if you know the source; leave the author off if it's original.
- Avoid inside references that only make sense to a handful of members — the
  quote may surface on any day.
